Overview

Zuppy ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and your pet's information. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we use and protect it when you use the Zuppy platform for emergency pet care and dog-walking services.

By using Zuppy's app or services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
Information we collect

Account & identity: Name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and address when you register.

Pet information: Pet's name, breed, age, weight, health notes, vaccination records, and emergency contacts you choose to provide.

Location data: Real-time GPS location to match you with the nearest available walker or emergency vet partner. Location is collected only when the app is active or a booking is in progress.

Payment information: Card details processed securely by our PCI-DSS compliant payment partner. Zuppy does not store full card numbers.

Usage data: App usage logs, session durations, device type, OS version, and crash reports to improve our service.

How we use your information
  • Match you with the nearest available dog walker within 10 minutes
  • Coordinate emergency pet care with partner vets and pet ambulance services
  • Process payments and send booking confirmations
  • Send real-time updates (walker location, ETA, walk completion reports)
  • Improve our app through anonymised analytics
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform safety

Data security & retention

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active, or up to 3 years after account deletion for legal compliance. Location data from completed bookings is anonymised after 90 days.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You may also withdraw consent for location tracking via your device settings. To exercise your rights, contact privacy@zuppy.app.

Cookies & tracking

The Zuppy app uses session cookies and analytics SDKs (Firebase Analytics) to understand feature usage. No third-party advertising trackers are used. You can opt out of analytics in Settings → Privacy.

Policy updates

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be notified via push notification and email at least 14 days before taking effect.
